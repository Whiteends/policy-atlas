01 / Principles

Evidence before score

The assessor records what was queried, what was returned and what could not be established. Recommendations are derived only after evidence collection completes. The tool does not equate the presence of a policy name with effective protection; scope, state, exclusions and controls matter.

02 / Signal states

Four outcomes for every control

  • Detected: sufficient configuration evidence supports the signal.
  • Missing: collection succeeded and the expected control was not found.
  • Not applicable: the control does not apply because of an explicit tenant condition.
  • Manual confirmation: Graph cannot reliably verify the control, permissions were insufficient, or the signal represents governance rather than configuration.

03 / Stage assignment

A weighted score, with critical safeguards

The 28 controls contribute a total of 100 points. Their weights reflect relative security impact and the breadth of exposure they address, so foundational protections carry more points than narrow or advanced capabilities.

A detected control earns its full weight. Missing, unknown, errored and manual-review controls earn no points until verified. A control confirmed as not applicable is removed from the available total, and the result is normalized back to 100.

Score bands are Stage 0: 0–24, Stage 1: 25–49, Stage 2: 50–69, Stage 3: 70–84 and Stage 4: 85–100. Critical gaps are reported separately as implementation-readiness blockers; they do not obscure or silently alter the numerical maturity score.

Implementation order is calculated separately. It considers security value, prerequisites, implementation effort and change blast radius; those delivery factors do not inflate the maturity score.

04 / Confidence

Unknown is not missing

Assessment confidence declines when required endpoints fail, permissions are unavailable or important signals require manual confirmation. A lower-confidence stage remains useful, but the report must show exactly which uncertainties could change the result.

05 / Recommendations

Prioritize risk reduction and readiness

Recommendations will consider severity, exposure, prerequisites, licensing and implementation effort. A missing foundational control should outrank an advanced feature even when both belong to the next maturity stage.

06 / Limitations

This is not a certified audit

The tool cannot prove operational effectiveness, change-management quality, alert response, user experience or control performance under attack. Microsoft Graph and licensing schemas also change. Findings require professional review before production changes.

07 / Provenance

Version every input to the result

Future snapshots will carry script, schema, maturity-model and policy-library versions. The report renderer will identify incompatible snapshots rather than silently interpreting an unknown format.
